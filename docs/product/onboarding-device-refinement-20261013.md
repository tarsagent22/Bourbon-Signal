# Onboarding refinement from the second device walkthrough

North Carolina shows its established strong coverage, even when collection is temporarily delayed. Onboarding uses only durable coverage strength; it never forwards operational health, summaries, or freshness text. The Census-derived state outline is geographic, with a separate qualitative coverage indicator. Requests appear for genuinely sparse or absent coverage.

The taste path offers named familiar bottles and shows artwork only for a chosen bottle. Every starter recommendation has short distillery-sourced editorial copy, linked in `shared/onboarding-bottles.ts`, plus an explanation tied to actual selected flavors or shared tasting notes. With no answer, it offers styles to try without claiming personalization. Catalog prices without a known bottle volume stay hidden.

A focused Shelf screen stays inside onboarding: choose unopened ownership, open ownership, or tasted without ownership. A rating is optional. It reuses collection mutations, version protection and fresh access checks; selecting a recommendation does not create ownership. The same picker serves experienced members. Bottle details keep one primary action and a quiet header back control.

Community examples are visibly marked as examples and use ABC context for NC. No invented retailer, price, member identity or live timestamp is shown. Alerts use the selected bottle or shelf choice when available. Both membership limits and Barrel Proof Community alerts are visible before selecting a plan. Store-localized price is shown on the purchase action. Free feed and points policies are unchanged.

No real glassware product photograph exists in the checked assets, so the established reward icon is retained at a smaller size. No fictitious product photo or member post is presented as real.

Verification: responsive real-component browser journeys cover coverage delays, keyboard/safe areas, ownership versus tasted records, recommendations, purchases and push-permission branches. Native device acceptance remains separate from these checks.
