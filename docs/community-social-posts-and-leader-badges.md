# Community photo posts and leader awards

Community uses a separate compact post card; Intel retains its existing renderer. Approved public Blob photos fit a 110–180px frame according to viewport height, retain their aspect ratio and open in a full-photo dialog. Bottle titles and captions stop at two lines; location stops at one. Names retain a separate immutable member/founder number. Up to three chosen earned badge icons open a list of their labels. Bad image requests show a fallback.

The Rewards badge collection shows four distinct dated awards: Most Active and Top Contributor for a calendar month, and Most Active and Top Contributor for a calendar year. Year winners are calculated from the year’s contributions, not the number of monthly trophies. Awards remain earned; no current-period provisional badge is shown. Tied leaders share recognition. No points are issued.

Calendar boundaries use America/New_York. Settlement happens seven days after the period closes. The first monthly competition is the month in which the schema is first installed; the first yearly competition is that calendar year. Annual scoring uses qualifying durable records from the entire year. Existing earlier months are not retrospectively awarded.

Monthly eligibility requires five qualifying contributions across three days. Yearly eligibility requires 25 across 12 days. Most Active ranks distinct active days. Top Contributor adds one per canonical in-store sighting, one extra for an approved photo, two extra for a helpful sighting (at least three distinct other members’ upvotes and a net three upvotes), and two per rewarded first-hand availability update. Canonical bottle/store/day duplicates count once, with at most three sightings and three availability updates per member per day. Pending/manual submissions, removed/rejected posts, self-votes, and contributors restricted for spam/deception are excluded. Awards and settlement markers are committed atomically under a transaction lock; retries do not award again. Explicit rejection of supporting posts or an active contributor restriction hides/revokes an award. Ordinary deletion after earning does not remove recognition.

Leader awards use separate storage and join the achievement summary and featured-badge ownership checks. They do not enter the Signal Points ledger or get recreated by ordinary rewards reconciliation. New posts include the member’s featured leader awards. Feed projections revalidate dated leader labels in one batch when present, so stale snapshots cannot display a revoked or another member’s award. Account deletion removes award records; backups include the complete three-table group once installed and support safety backups before migration.

Deploy the targeted schema after a verified encrypted safety backup with `node --env-file=.env.local scripts/migrate-community-leader-badges.mjs --apply`; `--check` is read-only. The backend adds a daily bearer-authenticated settlement cron returning aggregate counts only. Deploy the backend and compatible iOS OTA through the sole guarded release lane. No native dependency, permissions, app configuration or runtime change is included.

Validation: `npm run test:community-leaders` executes real PostgreSQL schema/functions in PGlite, including month/year independence, grace boundaries, ties, anti-spam, availability rewards, ownership, repeat runs and moderation. Existing reward/API/account/backup checks and complete mobile verification also run. The component fixture in `apps/mobile/scripts/community-preview` renders actual cards and badge collection with synthetic posts/awards. At 390×844 and 320×568 it tests overflow, bounded cards, full-photo dialogs and badge details. Browser fixtures and successful exports do not establish physical-iPhone interaction or OTA installation.
