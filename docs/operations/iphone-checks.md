# iPhone speed and notification checks

Use the installed production app. These checks are still pending until they are performed on an iPhone.

Record the iPhone model, iOS version, app version, date, network, and Low Power Mode setting. Use the same account and network for comparisons. A screen recording makes timings easier to compare; keep account details and notifications private.

## Speed

1. Close the app completely, then open it. Measure from tapping the icon until Home shows usable reports. Repeat three times.
2. Open Radar, Community, and My Shelf. Repeat each visit five times. Record the slowest visit and any frozen taps or scrolling.
3. Search for a bottle, open a report, and open the sighting form. Check whether typing and navigation respond immediately. Cancel the form without posting a test sighting.
4. Repeat once on cellular. Briefly disconnect the network: saved content should remain usable, and a failed refresh should offer a clear next step. Reconnect and refresh.

Record seconds and symptoms separately. API timings, exports, and automated tests do not measure iPhone rendering or scrolling. Treat these first runs as the baseline; compare later changes on the same device.

## Notifications

1. Confirm iOS allows notifications for Bourbon Signal and the app has phone alerts enabled. Choose an area with current inventory and either a watched bottle or all notable finds. Focus mode and Scheduled Summary can delay display; record their settings.
2. Use a real eligible alert. Leave the app in the background, then tap the notification. Confirm the correct bottle and location open, with the original report time.
3. Repeat with the app fully closed. Navigation should wait for sign-in and app readiness, then open the report once.
4. Tap an older notification after its report leaves the current feed. The sent report should remain readable; an unavailable destination should have a clear explanation.
5. Sign out, then tap a prior notification. Confirm another account's alert details are not shown. Sign back into the original account and retry.

For each notification, record the displayed time, bottle, location, app state, and what opened. Do not put device tokens or account identifiers in shared notes. A delivered provider receipt confirms provider acceptance, not visible delivery or successful navigation on the phone. Do not manufacture fresh inventory or send other members test alerts.
