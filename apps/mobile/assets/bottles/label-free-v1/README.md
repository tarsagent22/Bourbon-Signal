# Label-free artwork, batch 1

Five owner-approved generated shape masters cover 16 exact catalog entries in
`src/components/label-free-bottle-artwork.ts`. Each export preserves the approved
render and alpha, with proportional downsampling to at most 720 by 1080 pixels.
The five bundled images total 1,912,462 bytes. `provenance.json` records prompts,
shape reference paths, master/export hashes and export dimensions.

Names and edition information remain in the app's text. Reuse is an explicit
allowlist, never a brand prefix, inferred canonical key or catalog alias dump.
Eagle Rare 12/17/25, George T. Stagg, Blanton's Gold, Michter's American Whiskey
and other unreviewed forms are excluded from this batch. Generated forms are
illustrations, not an assurance of exact physical dimensions or legal clearance.

Approved artwork takes precedence over remote photos for these entries. Other
catalog entries retain their existing rendering until a subsequent batch.
Cabinet, grid and list views are static. Expanded My Shelf artwork uses a gentle
native-driven sway; it pauses in the background and honors Reduce Motion.

Validation: mobile verification (typecheck, native dependencies, iOS recovery,
357 mobile tests, 35 Astra tests, 3 browsing tests, release-readiness, Android/iOS
exports), plus a phone-width fixture rendering the production artwork component.
Physical iPhone acceptance remains a separate owner check after OTA publication.
