# App Store screenshot specification

Screenshots must come from the signed TestFlight candidate or a matching simulator build. Do not use AI imagery, fabricated inventory, private identifiers, personal email addresses, or owner/admin accounts.

## Required source set

Capture portrait iPhone screenshots at **1320 × 2868 px** for Apple’s 6.9-inch display slot. App Store Connect can scale this accepted set for smaller iPhone displays under Apple’s current screenshot rules. Reconfirm the accepted dimensions at submission time.

If Apple changes the required device slots, regenerate from the same approved scenes rather than stretching old screenshots.

## Approved scenes

1. **Fresh Signals, clearly sourced** — Signal Feed with two or three representative cards; no private identifiers.
2. **Exact details when evidence supports them** — Signal detail showing retailer, reported time, price/quantity when available, and the availability caveat.
3. **Access that follows your membership** — Account screen showing public Member/Founder label and effective feed access, plus privacy/support controls.
4. Optional: **Secure member access** — sign-in screen, only if a fourth screenshot improves the sequence; never show credentials or a verification code.

## Capture rules

- Use a dedicated review account with representative but non-sensitive data.
- Keep system status-bar time and carrier presentation consistent.
- Do not show “scout”; public identities are `Founder #N` or `Member #N`.
- Do not imply guaranteed quantity, orderability, nationwide coverage, alerts for free members, or native purchasing.
- Keep captions short, factual, and outside interactive UI elements.
- Inspect every final PNG together as one sequence before upload.
- Confirm text remains legible after App Store scaling and no screen contains loading, error, debug, or development-client chrome.

## Deferred until Apple access

Real iPhone/TestFlight screenshots are intentionally not fabricated on Windows. Capture and approve them after the signed candidate is available in TestFlight.
