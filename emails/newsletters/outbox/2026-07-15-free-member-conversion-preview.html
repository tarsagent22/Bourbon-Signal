I wanted to give you a clearer picture of what comes with your account. Free members can check the Drop Feed, run three Bottle Checks, and take part in Member Sightings. Paid members get the full feed, unlimited Bottle Check, and alerts for the bottles and places they care about.

WHAT’S NEW

A few things we’ve been working on.

Coverage has grown to 24 states. Thousands of store and location records now support the feed across the areas Bourbon Signal covers.

Release Radar is now live. It brings upcoming bottle release dates, lottery windows, events, and state guides into one calendar.

We’ve also started onboarding retailers. The goal is to let stores add bottle availability and events directly to Bourbon Signal.

If there’s a retailer you’d like to see on Bourbon Signal, reply to this email and tell me. I’m putting together the outreach list now.
