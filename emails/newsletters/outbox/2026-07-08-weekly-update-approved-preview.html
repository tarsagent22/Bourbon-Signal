Hey,

Chandler here — I wanted to send a quick update now that Bourbon Signal has been live for over a week.

First of all, thank you. The launch exceeded expectations, and I’m genuinely grateful for the nearly 150 members we gained in just the first week!

It’s still early, but Bourbon Signal is starting to do what it’s supposed to do: help bourbon hunters find better signals without digging through a bunch of noise. I work diligently every day to improve the quality and experience for all of you.

What’s new

Free members can now post sightings.

If you spot a useful bottle in the wild, you can now submit a sighting even if you're a free member. More member sightings add value for everyone. Free members have limited access to the member sightings feed as well.

Points are rolling out.

Sightings, badges, streaks, and eligible referrals earn Signal Points. Free members can accumulate points and badges but cannot redeem; paid members can redeem available catalog rewards in the Member Points drawer.

Action Points
Limited or unclassified sighting +10
Allocated sighting +20
Unicorn sighting +30

Manual bottle and store entry is live.

If Bourbon Signal doesn’t recognize something you type, you can add it to the database by filling out as much of the information form as possible. That means the product gets smarter as members use it—more bottles, better store context, and fewer missed signals over time.

Founder’s glasses are in the works.

I’m working on ordering and delivering the first batch of the Founding Member glasses as soon as possible. More on that soon.

If you signed up as a free member and have been waiting to see if Bourbon Signal would keep improving, this is a good time to come back and check it out!

Free vs. paid

Free members can explore Bourbon Signal, post sightings, submit missing bottle/store info, and start earning points.

Paid members unlock the deeper hunting tools: alerts, bottle tracking, state and area preferences, My Collection and Bourbon DNA, better Bottle Check context, and future rewards eligibility.
